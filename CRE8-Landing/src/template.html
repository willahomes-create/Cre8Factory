Questions

Before you ask.

Where exactly is CRE8? +

CRE8 is inside Novva Industrial Park, Sungai Buaya, Serendah. The North–South Expressway (E1) is reachable today via the Sungai Buaya Interchange. A planned connection to the Bukit Beruntung Interchange is targeted for Q1 2028.

What's the difference between Type A, B and C? +

Type A is a 0.99-acre intermediate lot with 22,833 sq ft built-up. Type B is a 1.20–1.24-acre intermediate lot with 25,865 sq ft built-up. Type C is a 1.19–1.25-acre corner lot with 25,844 sq ft built-up. All three include ground- and first-floor offices, a canteen, washrooms and a surau.

Can it handle heavy machinery? +

Yes. The production floor is designed for 3-tonne floor loading, with a 12-metre ceiling height and spacious setbacks for vehicle turning and loading.

Who is the developer? +

CRE8 Development Sdn Bhd, a subsidiary of Legacy Maker Berhad.

Is the viewing really free with no obligation? +

Yes. A viewing is simply the best way to judge the site, the scale and the access for yourself. There's no obligation to buy.
